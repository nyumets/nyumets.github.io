Understanding metastatic cancer

Measuring Stage IV cancer as it evolves.

Metastatic cancer changes clinically, radiographically, molecularly and genetically over years. NYUMets applies open data science to that complexity, releasing real-world longitudinal data and the tools to study it.

A joint project of NYU Grossman School of Medicine and NYU Center for Data Science.
